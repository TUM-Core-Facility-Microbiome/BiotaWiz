# Ask a question

Questions, ideas and experiences are welcome in
**[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/TUM-Core-Facility-Microbiome/BiotaWiz/discussions)**.

To get a quick answer, please include:
- your BiotaWiz version (**Settings › About**) and the TACTIC version (shown on the **Base** step),
- your operating system (Windows 10/11 or Linux distribution),
- the error message or code, and
- `Pipeline_log.txt` from your base directory (remove anything confidential first).
